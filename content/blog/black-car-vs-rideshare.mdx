Rideshare is great for a quick trip across town. For an early flight, a client meeting or a wedding, a booked black car usually wins. Here's why.

## The price is fixed

Rideshare prices move with demand, so the same trip can cost very different amounts at 5 a.m. or after a concert. A black car is quoted once, up front, and that's the price you pay.

## The car is booked, not found

With rideshare, you request a ride and hope someone accepts. With a black car, your chauffeur and vehicle are assigned ahead of time. You get their name the day before, and they arrive 15 minutes early.

## The car fits the trip

You choose the vehicle: a sedan for one or two people, an SUV for a family with luggage, a Sprinter for a group. Every car is cleaned before each ride and stocked with water and chargers.

## When rideshare makes sense

Short, unplanned trips where timing isn't critical. For everything else, especially anything involving a flight, it's worth booking ahead.
